Snap Unfollowers

Find the people you added on Snapchat who never added you back. Everything runs in this page; your file is not uploaded anywhere.

1. Get your Snapchat data

  1. Sign in at accounts.snapchat.com and open My Data.
  2. Tick Friends (you can untick everything else), choose JSON as the format, and submit.
  3. Snapchat emails you a link to a mydata~….zip. Download it. Inside is json/friends.json.

2. Open the file here

    3. Remove them in Snapchat

    There is no safe way to remove friends automatically, so tick each person off here as you do it in the app:

    1. Open Snapchat, tap your profile (top left), then My Friends.
    2. Search the username, open their profile and tap ⋯ (top right).
    3. Tap Manage Friendship → Remove Friend.

    Your export is a snapshot. If their Snap Score is hidden on their profile they still haven't added you back; if you can see it, they added you since the export, so skip them.
